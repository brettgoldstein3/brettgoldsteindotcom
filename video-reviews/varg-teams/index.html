good low budget feature launch

  • playful and authentic (esp the broll at the end)
  • great pacing (ADHD is happy!)
  • love the scroll stopper
  • good use of screen recordings
  • good captions (very necessary)

but

  • "from slash to spark is confusing"
  • can feel a little too low budget at times
  • as a video startup I partially expect them to have extremely high quality AI videos in all of their launches. show off the product!
  • would have made some words bigger to emphasize / cover for with accents

also I'm going to be honest about accents.

if you have a thick accent like they do, you need to speak slower/annunciate, make sure you have excellent and clear captions, and spend time doing accent training/coaching.

don't try to avoid this with AI or another speaker. founders need clear communication to be able to lead either way.

any founder with a thick accent that puts themselves out there on camera like this is a hero. not easy to do but its the right thing to do.

very cool feature though! many people have asked me for my video recipes recently. congrats team :)
