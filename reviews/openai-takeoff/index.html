OpenAI Takeoff

August 18, 2026 · 3/5

confusing brand marketing video

  • beautiful cinematography
  • unique but relatable characters
  • great product shots (more folks should emulate this)
  • perfectly builds energy with inspiring music

but

  • takeoff symbolism is confusing with characters mixing and going to space
  • some of the shots are meme-worthy (though this is supposed to be serious)
  • a little long and doesn't quite get me to a clear point

OAI's recent stuff has been brilliant but this wasn't. mostly because the metaphor is confused.

rockets taking off are a classic symbol for entrepreneurship, but it kinda felt like the girl was the only character it really related to (she was making a space game it looked like).

the characters should have either

  • had each of their ideas come fully to life (e.g. companion character walking into the room with the guy)
  • or none of the characters should have been building a space-related thing and all had the take off effect, but should have seen their ideas once hitting space

still an inspiring video!
