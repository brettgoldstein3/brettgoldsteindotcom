amazing commercial launch video

  • incredibly professional. most pro video I've seen for a startup this size
  • use and style of typography is great. streetwear style works for superpower very well
  • script is perfect
  • love the technical overlay - v aligned to superpower's brand
  • very consistent with their brand overall

but

  • didn't fully catch how superpower makes it possible
  • "a new health membership" is a terrible tagline

this looks like its supposed to be on TV and typically you don't want that on X but I think it works for superpower for a few reasons:

they are a startup, so having a very very polished commercial makes them seem more credible which attracts investors and hires

as a health product, trust is important, so again being super polished is useful here.

overall I think superpower has the strongest brand (and team behind it) in health tech. maybe all early stage startups. definitely up there with ramp and others with a video like this.

amazing work.
