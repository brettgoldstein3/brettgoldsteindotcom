Pally

July 30, 2026

really smart positioning, undercooked video

  • pretty clean animations and general quality for a $100 video (I was told)
  • script is solid. "lives in your texts" and "stop ghosting people" are both strong hooks
  • the general agent pivot is smart. start with the relatable pain (ghosting), then expand the product surface
  • clear CTA

but

  • a little too sepia tone for my taste
  • "introducing... a brand new pally" is weak. if i dont know what pally is, i dont care that its brand new
  • the feature list gets heavy. texts, DMs, flights, locked cards, browser automation. at some point my brain checks out. pick a couple to own.
  • matching outfits can read as try hard if the video doesnt earn it (the most legit founders never seem to do this)
  • "do basically anything else" a bold claim that you gotta back up.

I would not have released this video so soon after another general purpose iMessage agent did their launch. and on the same day as ANOTHER iMessage agent launch.

they nailed something the other didnt though - differentiation. messaging integration is killer.

congrats team
