nice acquisition announcement video

  • high quality shots
  • very good to reintroduce poke / interaction
  • very good to clearly say what is actually changing
  • love the unified logo/branding at the end
  • smart to reuse poke's most viral and memorable video

but

  • misses the biggest question people have "poke is pure consumer, devin is enterprise coding agents... why? is cognition going consumer? or is poke going enterprise"
  • kinda cheesy ending with nonsensical line "its been a long time"
  • music is borderline cheesy

I wish more companies would do acquisition announcement videos like this. truthfully, given how strange of a partnership this is, it was necessary in this case.

they hit the core questions and information very very well but I think folks are still confused where things are actually going. if this was an expensive talent acquisition or Devin is expanding to consumer.

overall very solid. congrats interaction!
