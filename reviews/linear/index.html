Linear

June 11, 2026 · 4/5

beautiful launch video

  • love how aligned to linear's brand this is. everything is perfectly dialed in.
  • music sets the perfect tone
  • really creative & original approach in general (esp with the projected text / UI)
  • great seeing real users
  • really authentic broll and user discussions
  • shots are really nice in general

but

  • would have got to the point of what they're launching faster.
  • need a scroll stopper. "linear is a shared context system for teams" as the first line is boring and confusing
  • the live speech is pretty awkward without an audience. who is Karri talking to? would have had a live audience reacting to the announcement.
  • would have had much clearer text for the specific things they're announcing. currently too hidden or hard to read.

think this is a great example of what a dark/chic brand can do with music to make the product feel uplifting/happy rather than scary (usually the case for dark brands)

the rule for looking off camera while talking is that you are either doing documentary style interview or there is a clear audience or person you're speaking to. v awkward if neither is true (in this case for linear).
