killer accelerator application launch video

- music sets the perfect seriousness and intensity and syncs up nicely (one of the best videos music-wise)
- clearly shows what's different about hf0 vs yc (very very legit second time founders)
- generally very engaging to watch because of how impressive the founders are and how organic the shots feel (not a drop of corporate try hard slop)

but
- would have started with one of the brain exploding stories (e.g. AIOS went from $ 90-300M revenue)
- might have color graded the video to match the intense music (some shots are too colorful)

when you have a monster competitor like hf0 has in yc, this is a great example on how to counter-position successfully through music and storytelling.

when I ran launch house (also a yc competitor), we also counter-positioned but in the opposite direction - more fun and cool (so bright / upbeat vibes).

I actually think the hf0 positioning is stronger. hf0 folks are very quirky and their demo day has standup-comedy-level "performances" but focusing on presenting their serious side like they do here makes the program feel very elite (which is what you need to attract elite founders).

congrats team!
