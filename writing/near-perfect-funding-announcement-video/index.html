near perfect funding announcement video

- very very original clipping style and aesthetic
- incredible script (maybe the best I've seen so far)
- music is so good and fits the vibe perfectly
- nice and short

but
- the vibe verryyyy dystopian
- would have introduced the founder earlier "this is my founder Ben, he mostly just hangs out because I do all the work"

the big issue here is the positioning is extremely tone def - though it could have been an "any press is good press" strategy.

it gives strong "AI doesn't need you any more" or even "AI is evil and taking over the world" energy with the voice modulation and graphics.

AI products (especially ones that automate jobs like this) need to position around giving people time back and helping individuals achieve their dreams. people are worried about AI for good reason and even a neutral tone can be off-putting.

this is why our brand leans heavily into beautiful blue skies, green fields, upbeat soul music and "organized so you don't have to be" - its happy!

don't know who directed and edited this video, but absolutely amazing work nonetheless

congrats ben and team
