pretty good launch advice thread

…if you’re silicon valley royalty

most founders have <10k followers, no tier 1 investors, and very little capital

so you need to be wayyyy scrappier

1. ALWAYS BE LAUNCHING - no one remembers your launch so you should launch over and over again. we launched 4 times over the last 18 months.

2. BUILD GOOD WILL - interact with big accounts on X for months before you launch. DM them, help them. don’t ask for anything until the week of launch. they’ll at least leave a comment. also warm up some customers with large followings / that love your product.

3. MAKE PEOPLE CARE - when no one cares about you or your startup, your video needs to say 4 things in the first 5-10 seconds
- what it does (with a bold statement)
- why it matters (social proof)
- who it’s for

4. USE INFLUENCERS - controversial, but anyone who doesn’t believe in influencer marketing is either unsuccessful or so privileged (with followers/influence) that they don’t need to. if you’re a nobody (99.99% of us are) you need to put some budget to influencer marketing to get the algorithms attention. put your ego aside. it’s a necessary evil unfortunately.
