founders fund absolutely mogged on this one

perfectly executed example of taking a popular format from another industry and transposing it onto tech

reality TV stars = tech celebrities (investors, founders, creators)
gameshow = games nerds play (mafia)

everything else is the same. the music, the editing, everything.

seems like the series is focused on mafia but you can do this with so many other formats
- endurance/athletic challenges = founder/investor biking, lifting, running
- jeopardy = tech industry quiz show
- late night talk shows = guests are tech industry folks, musical guests are tech adjacent musicians

its the exact insight TBPN had in creating "ESPN for tech" and why they were so successful

arguably, this is also why MTS from a16z hasn't taken off. it's not a perfect copy of an existing format like MAFIA or TBPN is. it feels like something in between a tech podcast and a TV news show.

I've said this for launch videos, but it's true for literally every kind of content:

rhyme with the greats

take what works in other domains and apply it to your own.
