great customer testimonial launch video

- these customer videos are so powerful. nothing speaks louder than people who love your product (and you can see it on their faces)
- great scroll stopper
- music during customer testimonials is nice
- good to note team's credibility
- good CTA
- nice and short

but
- music is very off when the founders start talking.
- founders' narration is weird (too serious, almost condescending or angry)
- would probably have lead with the $75M from Accel bit
- very low quality brand assets and editing
- "AI for everyone else" is weak af for what this thing does. "Your star employee that always knows what to do" or something like that would be better
- would have shown the product in action or explained how to set it up

its a pretty magical product and honestly the testimonials and fundraise eclipse the other bad parts of this video
