lovely aesthetics in this launch video

- one of the best uses of AI I've seen. not photorealistic so you don't feel deceived. and the aesthetic is really original
- music is perfectly lined up
- lots of movement to keep attention

but
- no idea who the founder is, what the product is, why it matters to me, etc.
- this could just as easily be any other social app's launch video. even a brand like Pepsi. looks like they just slapped their name on the beginning and end.
- gets tiring after a while, again because it doesn't say anything other than "check out this trippy video"

fundraise announcements are best used to attract talent and investors for your next round if your customers aren't on X.

videos like this don't really accomplish that. you can only announce once, so its best to just save it to when you really need it and do it right.
