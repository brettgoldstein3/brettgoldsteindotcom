gotta hand it to sam on this solid video

it has super engaging quick shots and music keeps your attention

most importantly, it celebrates their target customer (I think) who sam himself embodies completely - the sales bro

it covers basically every nostalgic moment in sales and sports (which sales guys love) and taps into their inner psyche (they want to get rich af and vacation in monaco)

its so on the nose, that it will probably put some people off.

for example, there is only ONE woman in the whole video (for 1 second at the end). so if you're a woman or even on the left side of the political spectrum, this video says "monaco isn't for you"

good startup brands pick a lane like this. great startup brands tap into the feelings of their customers and the broad zeitgeist (which now wholeheartedly embraces masculine sales guy energy like this again)

only thing I'd note is that while well executed, this is a very unoriginal format. sales guys aren't known to be creative geniuses, so I guess it checks out!
