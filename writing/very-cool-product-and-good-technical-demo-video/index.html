very cool product and good technical demo video

- music is fun
- cuts and speed are solid
- the low budget vibe here makes the product actually feel more impressive (sprezzatura). doesnt feel salesy at all.
- good to see the actual product featured so much
- nice an short)

but
- need to zoom into the text a lot more (most folks can't read that small/fast)
- would have more text explaining the product popping up (I imagine a black screen with white text between cuts like old film reels)
- doesn't say who its for
- could be more relatable with some quick clips of the founder interspersed

this feels like a great format for marketing to engineers, who care about seeing an actual product working & getting set up.

also can't understate how powerful an understated video with a killer product is. makes it feel 100x more impressive and make people want to support you (vs an overstated video gets a lot of hate).

I think if it had a few more elements from typical launch videos (text, narration, founder) this could be perfect.
