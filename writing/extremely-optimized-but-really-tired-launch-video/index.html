extremely optimized but really tired launch video

- perfectly optimized script
- scroll stopper
- lots of ADHD-friendly movement to keep attention
- nice and quick

but
- we have seen this exact format over and over and over again. try something new.
- this is not the first "AI head of growth" (please stop with these lines)
- animations + exaggerated script + salesy tone of the narration makes the whole thing feel fake and untrustworthy
- would have tried a different scene at the very least

this format will work on normies who havent seen a launch video before but if you're courting investors or other people active on X, it will backfire given how overplayed it is.

good example of the fact that content formats get very stale very quickly and while you should keep the same structure, you need to update the style to maintain trust and keep people engaged.
