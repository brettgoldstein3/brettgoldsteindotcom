well-produced product launch video

- nice trippy scroll stopper
- very cool mobile app shots
- good work on on-screen UI
- nice subtle animations and use of AI

but
- takes a longgggg time to say what the product is
- initial music is a touch on the eery side then later music sounds very cookie cutter and nothing matches up
- the serious tone mixed with the message of "other people are gonna beat you if you don't use our product" is not great messaging
- the shots of videos popping up on the screen is confusing. would have just picked one person to highlight "you can be like Alexis who XYZ" (and show her being really impressive) or you can keep doomscrolling (and show the most brain rot video)

subtle but interesting thought. the Eiffel tower in the background is nice, but it essentially communicates "this is european."

if they emphasized living a european-style offline easy life it would work, but the eery music + the general feelings people on X tend to have about europe (anti-free speech, pro-surveillance, 1984 energy) create for a bad mix for any consumer tech company
