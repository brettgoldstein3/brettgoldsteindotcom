cofounder social media etiquette:

- use the word "cofounder" as your role ("I'm cofounder of XYZ") but you can use "founder" as a noun for what you do ("I'm a founder")
- say "we" - "we started X" "we realized" (even solo founders should also do this)
- say "I" only for negative things
- announcements are always done by the CEO & reposted by the others (even if CEO has less followers)
- the CEO needs to be the loudest voice (help them if they struggle)
- its not a competition, but push each other to be better
- always give credit and hype up your cofounder/team. never ever subtweet anyone on your team
